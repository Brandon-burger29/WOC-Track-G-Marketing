# WOC Track G: Marketing

## KPMG-Sponsored IS Consulting Challenge

This repository contains our team's work for the KPMG-Sponsored IS Consulting Challenge in IS 3060 at the University of Utah. Our team selected Track G: Marketing and will use this repository to organize our work and project milestones throughout the semester.

## Problem Statement

Wasatch Outfitters Collective (WOC) currently tracks its advertising data separately across Instagram, Google, and email. Because this data is disconnected, WOC has difficulty determining how much it actually costs to gain a new customer and which marketing platforms are providing the most value.

## Preliminary Recommendation

WOC should combine its marketing data from Instagram, Google, and email into one centralized system in order to accurately calculate Customer Acquisition Cost and make better decisions about where to spend its marketing budget.
